Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the page and start studying.

What does “Again” do?

The card goes to the back of the pile so you see it again before the deck ends.

Where is my best score saved?

Only in your own browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server.

Can I add my own cards?

Yes. Open script.js and add new questions to the DECKS list.
